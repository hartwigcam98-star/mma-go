# Skill Spin MMA

Spin real UFC fighters' skills into one fighter, live his career, and fight it yourself in the 3D arcade.

**Install on iPhone:** open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and works offline.

Built from the Skill Spin MMA artifact (one self-contained `index.html`). Saves are kept in this site's browser storage on
your device. Motion capture credits: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (mocap.cs.cmu.edu); Bandai Namco Research
Motion Dataset (CC BY-NC 4.0); animations from Mixamo (converted joint data only).
